Americas Bean-to-Bar and Chocolatier Competition Winners 2020-21

  • 5 March, 2021
  • Winners

The International Chocolate Awards is pleased to announce the Winners of the 2020-21 Americas Bean-to-bar and Chocolatier Competition. (We’ve renamed our 2020 competitions as 2020-21 because of COVID related delays. The next season will be 2021-22.)

World Final Phase #3 – enter by March 18th, 2021

Winners should enter the World Final 2020-21 directly. Due to the deadline to complete the 2020-21 season, there is a short window for Americas winners to participate. Please see the World Final Phase #3 entry page for full details.

Entries should be sent to our European partner in Hannover, Germany, to arrive by March 19th, 2021.  Please get in touch with us if you need help and please follow the customs advice you will receive once you enter, this will help to ensure that your package arrives without delays. We will be flexible with shipping times if we possibly can.

International Judges

The judging panel for Americas included regular USA judges, along with pastry chefs, restaurateurs, journalists and other specialists from the New York area and members of our international Grand Jury panel.

All judging is anonymised, included at the Grand Jury final session, where Golds, Silver and Bronze prizes are awarded by private voting according to the 2020 Grand Jury rules.

All Gold, Silver and Bronze winners in this competition are eligible to enter the World Final.
